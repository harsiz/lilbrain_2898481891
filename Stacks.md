Stacks are literally just data that is blazingly fast because it's restricting the operations you can do. It's like stripping down a list.

E.g:

`LIFO` (Last In, First Out). This just means the last item that is put in the stack, is the first one to come out.

e.g:

```
Burger
Sandwidch
Chips
```

If I put in Burger last, it's the first one to come out if i grabbed it.
